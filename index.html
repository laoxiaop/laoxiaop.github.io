What this application is for

Health Data Tracker is a personal record-keeping tool. Its purpose is to gather the owner's own health and fitness data from Google's health service, keep it in one place on the owner's own computer, and show it back as trend charts and longitudinal summaries — so that the owner can discuss their own history with a doctor, spot long-term changes, and keep their records complete.

It is not a commercial product, not a public service, not a medical device, and it is not offered to or usable by anyone other than its owner. It does not diagnose, treat, or give medical advice, and it does not accept registrations.

What it does, step by step

  • The owner opens the application on their own Windows computer and authorises it once through Google's own OAuth consent screen.
  • The application then reads the owner's own Google Health data through the Google Health API, using only the read-only permissions the owner approved (see the table below).
  • The data is written to a local database file on the owner's computer, together with the original files exported from Google.
  • The application renders local trend charts and a browsable dashboard from that local file. Nothing is uploaded, published, or sent anywhere.

Which Google user data it accesses, and why

Google API permission (scope)Data coveredWhy the application needs it
googlehealth.activity_and_fitness.readonly Steps, distance, calories, active minutes, exercise sessions To show activity and exercise trends over time.
googlehealth.health_metrics_and_measurements.readonly Heart rate, weight, body composition and other body measurements To chart measured health values alongside the owner's own medical test results.
googlehealth.sleep.readonly Sleep sessions, sleep stages and duration To chart sleep duration and timing over time.
googlehealth.location.readonly Location tracks attached to the owner's own exercise sessions To display the route and distance of the owner's own workouts.

Every permission is read-only. Access is bounded by the consent the owner granted at accounts.google.com, and the owner can revoke it at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

How the data is used

  • Only for the owner's own personal tracking. No other purpose, no other person.
  • Never sold, rented, shared or disclosed to any third party, advertiser, data broker or analytics provider.
  • Never used for advertising, profiling, credit, insurance or employment decisions.
  • Never used to train or improve AI/ML models, and never fed to any third-party model or service.
  • No generative AI at all. The application does not generate, edit or distribute AI-generated images, video or audio of any person. It never creates or distributes AI-generated non-consensual intimate imagery (AI NCII) or any other synthetic intimate content, and it never processes Google user data for that purpose.

Use of Google APIs and of data received from them complies with the Google APIs Terms of Service and the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
